Cookie Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This page explains how gpatouris.com uses cookies and similar technologies. In short: this website does not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies.

01What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website can store in your browser. Some are needed for a site to work, while others track visitors or show targeted ads. Under EU and Cypriot rules, only strictly necessary cookies can be set without your consent.

02What this website uses

TypeUsed?Details
Strictly necessaryOnly if neededThe site does not need to store cookies to display its pages or send a booking request.
Analytics / statisticsNoWe do not use Google Analytics or similar tools.
Advertising / social trackingNoWe do not use advertising pixels or retargeting.

Because we set no tracking cookies, this site does not show a cookie consent banner.

03Third-party content

  • Google Fonts: text fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which means your IP address is sent to Google when a page loads.
  • Maps: the job-location pin on the booking page loads map data from a third-party map provider, which may receive your IP address and the area you view.
  • Social and messaging links: links to Facebook, WhatsApp and Viber take you to those services, which have their own cookie and privacy policies. Following a link is your choice.

04Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Blocking them will not stop you from using this website.

05If this changes

If we later add analytics or any non-essential cookies, we will first add a consent banner that lets you accept or refuse them, and update this page.
