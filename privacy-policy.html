Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data is collected when you use gpatouris.com, request a booking, or contact George Patouris about forklift and telehandler hire in Limassol, Cyprus, and what happens to it. We keep it to what is needed to do the job.

01Who is responsible for your data

George Patouris — forklift & telehandler hire, Limassol, Cyprus

George Patouris is the data controller for the data described below.

02What we collect

  • Booking request details: your name, phone number, email (if provided), the requested date, time and duration, the job location you pin or describe, and any notes you write.
  • Messages and calls: what you send us by phone, WhatsApp, Viber, email or Facebook, including your contact details and any photos or locations you share.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting provider may process your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in server logs, for security and to deliver the site.

We do not ask for payment card details on this website, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or automated decision-making.

04Who we share it with

Your data is shared only where needed to run the service:

  • Website hosting and form-handling providers that deliver this site and pass booking requests to us.
  • Map services used for the job-location pin, which may receive the location you select.
  • Messaging platforms (WhatsApp, Viber, Facebook, email providers) when you contact us or we reply to you. Their own privacy policies apply to those services.
  • Accountants or authorities, only where the law requires it.

Some of these providers are based outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, transfers rely on safeguards recognised under GDPR, such as an adequacy decision or Standard Contractual Clauses.

05How long we keep it

  • Booking requests and messages that do not lead to a job: deleted once the enquiry is closed, normally within 12 months.
  • Records of completed jobs and invoices: kept for the period Cypriot accounting and tax law requires.
  • Server logs: kept by the hosting provider for a short period under its own policy.

06Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to how it is used, and receive a copy in a portable format. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. To use any of these rights, email info@gpatouris.com; we will reply within one month.

If you believe your data has been mishandled, you can complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy). We would appreciate the chance to put it right first.

07Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and access to booking details is limited to George Patouris. No system is perfectly secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information you do not need to share.

08Cookies and links

See our Cookie Policy. Links to Facebook, WhatsApp and Viber lead to third-party services with their own privacy practices.

09Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
